## External replay contract and source anchors

The pinned [engine replay implementation](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Pikafish/blob/9b963f727983a1d9308e0dca48b39c802b8e75a2/src/engine.cpp#L158-L185) interprets a five-character suffix according to whether the moved piece is still dark, and six characters as mover reveal plus captured-dark identity. [Move parsing](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Pikafish/blob/9b963f727983a1d9308e0dca48b39c802b8e75a2/src/uci.cpp#L597-L604) searches legal moves by the first four characters. If a move is rejected, replay silently stops. Therefore `readyok` alone never proves a history was accepted: compare the labeled FEN after **every prefix**, using the [debug command](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Pikafish/blob/9b963f727983a1d9308e0dca48b39c802b8e75a2/src/uci.cpp#L131-L149). No search or evaluation-network load is requested by these transcript commands.

The [chase helper](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Pikafish/blob/9b963f727983a1d9308e0dca48b39c802b8e75a2/src/position.cpp#L973-L1183) needs a separate external diagnostic for qualifying physical targets and explicit ply-zero verdicts. Ordinary root search scores are insufficient. The no-move fixtures distinguish this helper from a complete referee: below 80 with no repetition history it returns no rule verdict even when there are no legal moves. Complete adjudication must separately handle mate/stalemate.

Additional source-inferred probe: replay decrements a captured pool through the [mutable rest-piece accessor](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Pikafish/blob/9b963f727983a1d9308e0dca48b39c802b8e75a2/src/position.h#L294-L296), whereas [state initialization](https://github.com/official-pikafish/Pikafish/blob/9b963f727983a1d9308e0dca48b39c802b8e75a2/src/position.cpp#L247-L288) hashes pool counts. Compare an incrementally replayed final key with a fresh load of its final FEN. A stale-key discrepancy is a hypothesis, not a confirmed runtime bug; final-FEN equality alone would not settle it. Fresh loading loses history, so compare state keys separately from repetition verdicts.

Keep any GPL diagnostic modification in the separate opponent checkout/worktree and record its diff/binary hash. No external implementation has been copied into our code, and no native verdict, game result or rating is claimed here.
